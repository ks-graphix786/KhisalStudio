How we work

Four steps. No mystery.

We have kept this the same since day one, because it is the part clients tell us they like most.

STEP 01

You tell us the goal

A short call or a voice note. What you sell, who you are talking to, and what working looks like.

STEP 02

We send a plan

Scope, timeline and a fixed price. No hourly surprises, no vague it depends.

STEP 03

We build it

First version in a few days. You get to see it early, not at the very end.

STEP 04

Revisions, then handover

Two rounds of changes included. You get the files, the logins and a short walkthrough.
