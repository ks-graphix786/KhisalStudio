About us

A small studio that would rather do four things well.

Khisal Studio started as one designer taking on freelance work. It is now a small team covering design, video, web and automation, which means you can brief one place instead of four.

Where we came from

Started in 2022, still doing the work ourselves.

Our founder, Khisal Ahmad, began as a graphic designer and video editor in March 2022. Hundreds of logos, banners, thumbnails and edits later, the work grew past what one person could carry, so the studio did too.

We have kept the same habit from those early days: we do the work ourselves rather than farming it out. If you brief us on a reel, the person editing it is someone who has been editing for years, not a freelancer we found last week.

What changed is the toolkit. We now build AI into the workflow, for generating scenes that would be expensive to film, for cutting rough edits faster, and for the automation systems that answer your leads while you are busy.

How we think

Three things we will not compromise on.

01

Plain & Honest answers

If a project is not a good fit, we say so before you pay anything. If a cheaper option would do the job, we will point at it. It costs us a job now and then, and it is why clients come back.

02

Files you own

You get the working files, the logins and the source, not a locked export. Nothing we build for you should require us to stay in the loop forever.

03

Deadlines that hold

We quote a date we can actually hit, then hit it. If something slips on our side, you hear it from us early, not the morning it was due.
